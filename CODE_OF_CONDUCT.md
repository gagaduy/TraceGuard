# Code of Conduct

TraceGuard contributors are expected to create a professional, safe, and inclusive environment in every project space.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and acknowledge uncertainty or mistakes.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, abilities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Protect confidential reports, personal information, and security-sensitive details.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project events, and public representation of the TraceGuard community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an ordinary moderation concern, contact the repository owner through the contact route on the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/gagaduy). If the report contains sensitive or safety-critical information, use the repository's [private security advisory form](https://github.com/gagaduy/TraceGuard/security/advisories/new) and mark it as a conduct report.

Maintainers will acknowledge a report as soon as practical, limit disclosure to people needed for review, hear affected parties where safe, and document an outcome. Responses may include a clarification, warning, temporary restriction, removal of content, or permanent exclusion. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in a review is prohibited.
